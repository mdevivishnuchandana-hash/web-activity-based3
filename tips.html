Tips for Healthy Living

Eat Fruits

Include a variety of fresh fruits in your meals.

Eat Vegetables

Include different coloured vegetables in your diet.

Drink Water

Drink water regularly throughout the day.

Reduce Excess Sugar

Limit sugary drinks and foods with added sugar.

Eat Balanced Meals

Include suitable sources of carbohydrates, protein, healthy fats, vitamins and minerals.

Stay Active

Make time for regular physical activity and adequate sleep.

Healthy habits make a positive difference.

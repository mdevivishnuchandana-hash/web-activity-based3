Popular Recipes

Fruit Salad

Ingredients: Apple, banana, papaya.

Method: Wash, chop and mix the fruits.

Calories: Approximately 120-180 kcal per serving.

Vegetable Salad

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot, lemon.

Method: Chop the vegetables and mix with lemon juice.

Calories: Approximately 50-100 kcal per serving.

Oats Porridge

Ingredients: Oats, milk or water, banana.

Method: Cook oats with milk or water and add banana.

Calories: Approximately 200-300 kcal per serving.

Vegetable Upma

Ingredients: Rava, carrot, peas, onion and water.

Method: Cook the vegetables and rava together.

Calories: Approximately 200-300 kcal per serving.
